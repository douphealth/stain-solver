A FEW THINGS TO KNOW / 05

Care before
confidence.

Good judgment can save more than a clever cleaning hack.

Can I treat something labelled “dry clean only”? +

A universal home treatment isn't safe. Blot off fresh excess gently without rubbing, and contact the item manufacturer or a professional cleaner. The solver applies a dry-clean-only stop rule.

What's the difference between sofa codes W, S and X?+

W relates to water-based cleaners, S to cleaning-solvent processes, W/S to either according to the maker, and X generally calls for vacuuming only. Check the exact furniture label before treatment.

What if I already tried the dryer?+

Add the “already machine-dried or ironed” option to your plan. Heat-exposed stains may be harder to remove. Avoid additional heat while the mark remains.

Are the methods guaranteed to work?+

No. Results depend on dye, fibre, finish, earlier treatment, and time. This tool offers safer first steps and a clear stop signal, not guaranteed restoration.
