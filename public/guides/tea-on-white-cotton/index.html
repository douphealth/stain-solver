Tea on white cotton.

For a washable, colourfast item, the steps below follow the stain type and fabric label. Skip anything the label forbids. Our first move: Blot with a clean white absorbent cloth. Work gently; do not rub the spill wider.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Blot with a clean white absorbent cloth. Work gently; do not rub the spill wider.

Why white cotton changes the answer

White cotton is usually launderable, but the care symbol still controls wash temperature and which products may be used. White fabric does not automatically tolerate chlorine bleach. Tea tannins may leave visible colouring as liquid dries.

Stain type: Tannin / beverage pigment. Tea contains colour compounds that can leave a mark after evaporation; treat like a washable beverage stain where allowed.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Move the beverage out with cool water

    Rinse from the back under cool water, or gently sponge with cool water if the garment label permits.

  2. 2

    Pre-treat with labelled laundry detergent

    Use a small amount of laundry detergent or a pretreatment marked safe for this fabric. Follow the cleaner’s contact time and rinse thoroughly.

  3. 3

    Launder once, then inspect

    Wash at the label-approved temperature with laundry detergent. If considering an oxygen laundry product for a persistent mark, check BOTH the textile’s bleach symbol and the cleaner instructions; never assume white means bleach-safe. Check the stain while the garment is still damp; air-dry rather than tumble-dry until it is gone.

What not to do

  • Do not iron or tumble-dry while the mark remains.
  • Never mix bleach with another cleaner, including acids or ammonia.
  • Do not skip a hidden-place dye/finish test.
Stop condition: Stop if the care label forbids treatment, dye transfers, fibre weakens, or cleaning spreads the mark.

What if it already dried or went through the dryer?

A cool-water rinse and fabric-safe pretreatment are good first choices on washable garments. A dried or heat-exposed stain can require different handling, and removal may be incomplete. In the interactive result, also select “already machine-dried or ironed” if applicable. The care-label restriction still takes priority.
