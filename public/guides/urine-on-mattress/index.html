Urine smell in a mattress.

The steps below follow the stain type and the item’s care label. Skip anything the label forbids. Our first move: Strip the bed and blot up as much urine as you can with dry towels, pressing straight down. The less liquid left in the foam, the less odour later.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Strip the bed and blot up as much urine as you can with dry towels, pressing straight down. The less liquid left in the foam, the less odour later.

Why this stain needs its own approach

Urine soaks through the cover into padding and foam, where it can't be rinsed out. The odour comes from compounds left as it dries, so the aim is to break those down and dry the mattress fully.

Enzyme cleaners made for urine break down the odour-causing compounds. Baking soda then absorbs remaining moisture and smell.

Stain type: Urine, in a surface that can't be laundered.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Blot, don't soak

    Press dry towels onto the wet area until little more comes up. Don't add water.

  2. 2

    Apply a urine enzyme cleaner

    Test on a hidden area, then mist or dab on enough to reach as deep as the urine went. Leave it for the time on its label, then blot.

  3. 3

    Absorb with baking soda

    Once the surface is only slightly damp, cover the area with baking soda and leave it for several hours or overnight, then vacuum it up.

  4. 4

    Dry completely

    Leave the mattress uncovered with good airflow, or use a fan, until fully dry before remaking the bed.

What not to do

  • Do not soak the mattress; trapped moisture causes mould and lingering smells.
  • Do not use ammonia-based cleaners; their smell resembles urine.
  • Never mix bleach with ammonia, vinegar or other cleaners.
Stop condition: Stop if the mattress maker forbids wet cleaning, the fabric changes colour, or moisture is soaking deep into the foam.

What if it already dried?

Old urine odour usually needs repeat enzyme treatments that reach the padding. A waterproof mattress protector prevents the next accident from reaching the mattress.
