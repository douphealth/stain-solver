Foundation on clothes.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Scrape off any excess with a blunt knife or the edge of a card. For powder foundation, shake and brush it off dry before using any water.

Why this stain needs its own approach

Liquid and cream foundations combine oils with pigment. The oil part needs a grease-cutting agent; the pigment part may need a stain pre-treatment afterwards.

Adding water to powder foundation first can turn it into a paste that soaks in, so remove as much as you can dry.

Stain type: Oil plus pigment.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Remove the excess

    Lift liquid foundation with a blunt edge, or brush powder off dry.

  2. 2

    Work in clear dish liquid

    Where the care label allows washing, dab a little clear dish liquid into the mark and work it gently with your fingertips. Leave it for 5 to 10 minutes.

  3. 3

    Rinse and pre-treat the colour

    Rinse with cool water. If a tint remains, apply an enzyme laundry pre-treatment and leave it as its label says.

  4. 4

    Wash and check

    Wash at the care label's temperature and check before drying.

What not to do

  • Do not rub hard; it pushes pigment deeper.
  • Do not use heat until the mark is gone.
  • Do not use makeup wipes on delicate fabrics; their oils can leave a ring.
Stop condition: Stop if the fabric is silk, wool or dry-clean only, or the colour shifts in a hidden test.

What if it already dried?

Dried foundation usually needs the dish-liquid step repeated, with a longer wait before rinsing.
