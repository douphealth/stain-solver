THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Lift excess solids or oily material with a spoon or the edge of a dull tool. Do not rub.

Why fabric sofa changes the answer

Fabric upholstery must be treated according to its cleaning code. A missing code is a reason to pause, not a reason to guess with water. Chocolate combines solids and oils: lift the excess before cleaning.

Stain type: Fat + solids + pigment. Chocolate combines fats and particles. Lift solids first, then treat residual oils on washable fabrics.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Blot or lift the excess

    Lift excess solids or oily material with a spoon or the edge of a dull tool. Do not rub.

  2. 2

    Find the furniture code

    Check the tag beneath a cushion or frame. Do not apply water or a cleaner until you identify W, W/S, S or X.

  3. 3

    No legible code? Pause here

    Ask the maker or a professional upholstery cleaner rather than guessing with household recipes.

What not to do

  • Do not rub, scrub or abrade the spot.
  • Never mix bleach with another cleaner, including acids or ammonia.
  • Do not add water before confirming W, W/S, S or X.
Stop condition: Stop for an unknown, S or X code before wet treatment. Stop if colour transfers, a water ring appears or filling is saturated.

What if it already dried or went through the dryer?

Avoid spreading melted chocolate by rubbing. A dried or heat-exposed stain can require different handling, and removal may be incomplete. In the interactive result, also select “already machine-dried or ironed” if applicable. The care-label restriction still takes priority.
