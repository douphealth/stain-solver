THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Blot, don’t soak. A mattress cannot be rinsed through, so use small amounts of cold liquid and press it out with dry towels each time.

Why this stain needs its own approach

A mattress traps moisture in its padding and foam. Over-wetting can spread the stain, leave a ring and encourage mould, so every step uses as little liquid as possible.

Blood is a protein stain: cold water and enzyme cleaners help, heat sets it. Check the mattress label or maker’s care instructions first, because some covers and memory foams have their own restrictions.

Stain type: Protein, on a surface that cannot be laundered.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Protect your hands

    Wear gloves for another person’s blood or blood from an unknown source.

  2. 2

    Blot with cold water

    Dampen a white cloth with cold water and press it onto the stain, then press with a dry towel. Work from the outside in and repeat.

  3. 3

    Use an enzyme cleaner sparingly

    Apply an enzyme stain remover approved for upholstery or mattresses, following its label and dwell time. Blot it out with a damp cloth, then a dry one.

  4. 4

    Stubborn marks on white fabric: hydrogen peroxide, after a test

    Dab 3% hydrogen peroxide onto a hidden area first. If the fabric is unaffected, dab a little on the stain, let it fizz, then blot dry. Do not saturate.

  5. 5

    Dry completely

    Leave the mattress uncovered with good airflow, or use a fan, until it is fully dry before putting bedding back on.

What not to do

  • Do not pour water or cleaner onto the mattress.
  • Do not use hot water or a steam cleaner on fresh blood.
  • Never combine hydrogen peroxide with bleach or ammonia.
Stop condition: Stop if the maker’s instructions forbid wet cleaning, the fabric changes colour, or moisture is soaking into the foam.

What if it already dried?

Dried blood needs repeated short applications of enzyme cleaner and patience. A faint mark may remain; a fitted waterproof mattress protector prevents the next one.
