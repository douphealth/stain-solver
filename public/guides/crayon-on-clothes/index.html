Crayon on clothes.

The steps below follow the stain type and the item’s care label. Skip anything the label forbids. Our first move: Scrape off any lumps of crayon with a blunt knife. If a crayon went through the dryer, check the drum before running another load.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Scrape off any lumps of crayon with a blunt knife. If a crayon went through the dryer, check the drum before running another load.

Why this stain needs its own approach

Crayons are wax with pigment. The wax lifts like candle wax; the pigment then needs an oil-cutting pre-treatment.

A crayon melted in the dryer can mark a whole load and leave wax on the drum that transfers to the next load.

Stain type: Wax with pigment.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Scrape the wax

    Chill with an ice pack if soft, then flake off the crayon.

  2. 2

    Lift the wax film

    If the label allows ironing, press the area between paper towels with a low, dry iron, changing the paper as it absorbs colour.

  3. 3

    Pre-treat the pigment

    Work clear dish liquid or a laundry pre-treatment into the mark and leave it for a few minutes.

  4. 4

    Wash and check

    Wash at the warmest temperature the label allows and check before drying.

What not to do

  • Do not tumble-dry stained items until the colour is gone.
  • Do not iron fabrics whose label forbids it.
  • Do not run another load until any wax is cleaned off the dryer drum.
Stop condition: Stop if the fabric reacts to heat or colour spreads into the surrounding fabric.

What if it already dried?

For a dryer load, treat each item separately with the same steps. Cool the drum and wipe wax off it following the dryer maker's instructions before using it again.
