Sweat marks on white cotton.

For a washable, colourfast item, the steps below follow the stain type and fabric label. Skip anything the label forbids. Our first move: Check whether this is residue or permanent fabric fading. Do not scrub discoloured fibres.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Check whether this is residue or permanent fabric fading. Do not scrub discoloured fibres.

Why white cotton changes the answer

White cotton is usually launderable, but the care symbol still controls wash temperature and which products may be used. White fabric does not automatically tolerate chlorine bleach. Sweat, skin oils and deodorant can leave different marks.

Stain type: Body oils + deodorant residue. Yellowing can involve body oils, deodorant residue or actual dye changes. Dye damage cannot be washed out.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Check for dye damage

    Compare the mark to nearby fabric. A yellowed residue may respond to treatment; a faded or bleached patch may be permanent.

  2. 2

    Target clothing residue

    Use a little liquid laundry detergent or labelled prewash product; for heavy deodorant buildup, ACI suggests a 5–10 minute pretreatment before washing.

  3. 3

    Launder once, then inspect

    Wash at the label-approved temperature with laundry detergent. If considering an oxygen laundry product for a persistent mark, check BOTH the textile’s bleach symbol and the cleaner instructions; never assume white means bleach-safe. Check the stain while the garment is still damp; air-dry rather than tumble-dry until it is gone.

What not to do

  • Do not iron or tumble-dry while the mark remains.
  • Never mix bleach with another cleaner, including acids or ammonia.
  • Do not skip a hidden-place dye/finish test.
Stop condition: Stop if the care label forbids treatment, dye transfers, fibre weakens, or cleaning spreads the mark.

What if it already dried or went through the dryer?

If perspiration has changed the fabric dye itself, cleaning may not restore the colour. A dried or heat-exposed stain can require different handling, and removal may be incomplete. In the interactive result, also select “already machine-dried or ironed” if applicable. The care-label restriction still takes priority.
