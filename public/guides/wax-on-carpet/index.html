Candle wax on carpet.

The steps below follow the stain type and the item’s care label. Skip anything the label forbids. Our first move: Let the wax harden completely, then break and lift as much as you can before using any heat.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Let the wax harden completely, then break and lift as much as you can before using any heat.

Why this stain needs its own approach

Most of a wax spill sits on top of the pile and comes off once it is hard. Only the thin layer around the fibres needs heat.

Many carpets are synthetic, and nylon or polypropylene can glaze or melt under a hot iron. Low heat, a barrier of paper and constant movement keep it safe. Coloured wax can leave dye behind, which needs a separate spot clean.

Stain type: Wax, sometimes with dye.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Harden and break it

    Press an ice pack in a bag onto the wax until it is brittle, then break it up and lift it out with a blunt knife or spoon. Vacuum the flakes.

  2. 2

    Lift the rest with low heat

    Lay a plain brown paper bag or a few sheets of paper towel over the spot. Press with an iron on a low setting, no steam, for a few seconds at a time, keeping it moving. Move to a clean area of paper as it absorbs wax.

  3. 3

    Treat any dye mark

    If coloured wax leaves a tint, blot with a carpet spot cleaner suitable for your carpet, after a test in a hidden area.

  4. 4

    Fluff and dry

    Let the area dry, then brush the pile back up with your fingers.

What not to do

  • Do not use a high iron setting or hold the iron still.
  • Do not use printed paper or newspaper: the ink can transfer.
  • Do not rub soft wax into the pile.
Stop condition: Stop if the fibres look shiny, stiff or melted, or the colour changes; call a professional carpet cleaner.

What if it already dried?

Old wax is easier than fresh, because it is already hard. Freeze, break and lift it, then use the low-heat paper method for the residue.

A QUICK MATERIAL CHECK

Before you treat it

Check the carpet’s fibre if you know it. Wool and synthetics both need low heat, and wool needs gentle cleaners.
