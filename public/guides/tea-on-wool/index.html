Tea on wool.

Wool has separate fibre and wash rules. Ordinary detergents, enzymes and rough handling may damage it. Our first move: Blot with a clean white absorbent cloth. Work gently; do not rub the spill wider.

THE SHORT ANSWER

START HERE

Blot with a clean white absorbent cloth. Work gently; do not rub the spill wider.

Why wool changes the answer

Wool can be damaged by inappropriate detergents or rough handling. A washable wool label changes the options; dry-clean-only wool should be handled by a specialist. Tea tannins may leave visible colouring as liquid dries.

Stain type: Tannin / beverage pigment. Tea contains colour compounds that can leave a mark after evaporation; treat like a washable beverage stain where allowed.

A careful step-by-step approach

  1. 1

    Lift or blot without rubbing

    Blot with a clean white absorbent cloth. Work gently; do not rub the spill wider.

  2. 2

    Confirm the wool label

    If it specifies professional dry cleaning, or this is lined/structured wool, stop home treatment now.

  3. 3

    Use only wool-specific care when washable

    For washable wool only: patch-test first, then gently dab with a lint-free cloth lightly dampened with cool water and wool-approved detergent diluted as directed. Avoid saturation, heat and aggressive rubbing.

  4. 4

    Remove residue gently and dry correctly

    Blot with a barely damp clean cloth and dry as the label instructs. Consult a wool-care professional if a ring or residue remains.

What not to do

  • Do not rub, scrub or abrade the spot.
  • Do not use ordinary enzyme stain removers, bleach or hot water on delicate fibres without maker approval.
  • Do not skip a hidden-place dye/finish test.
Stop condition: Stop for dry-clean-only, structured or lined garments, water rings or dye movement.

What if it already dried or went through the dryer?

A cool-water rinse and fabric-safe pretreatment are good first choices on washable garments. A dried or heat-exposed stain can require different handling, and removal may be incomplete.
