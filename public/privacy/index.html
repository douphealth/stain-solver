PRIVACY / IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Your mess. Your business.

No account. No tracking pixels. No stain history uploaded to our servers.

What stays on your device

When you choose to save a stain plan, its selections (stain type, surface, timing, care-label and cleaning-code options, and recovery context) are stored in your browser's local storage. If you set a default material, that preference is also saved locally. Checklist ticks and outcome notes are not sent to a server. You can clear saved plans from the tool at any time, or clear this website's storage in your browser settings.

What we don't collect

The app has no sign-in, advertising scripts, third-party analytics, forms or user-profiling APIs. It does not request your camera, contacts or location. It does not upload stain information, checklists, safety-check selections, screenshots or downloaded plan images. Copy, share, print and PNG export are handled by your browser. Shared links include selected options in the URL: when someone opens one, those options may be visible to the recipient and Cloudflare in the request URL. Avoid sharing links containing details you consider private.

Necessary technical requests

Visiting any website necessarily sends a request to its host. Cloudflare Pages serves the site and may process standard technical request information under Cloudflare's own terms and policies. External source links leave Stain Solver only if you choose to open them. The site does not load third-party fonts, external images or tracking scripts.

Offline mode

If supported by your browser, a service worker saves core pages and tools after a successful visit. Saved pages may continue working offline; source websites need a connection. You can delete the site storage and service worker via browser settings. When a new version is deployed, cached app assets update on a subsequent visit while online.

Affiliate links

No affiliate links are included in this release. If shopping links are ever added, paid links should be clearly identified and should not alter cleaning guidance.

Important caveat

This is a household guidance tool, not a guarantee of fabric restoration. Follow the item's care label, the product manufacturer's directions, and professional advice for valuable or delicate materials.

Questions about our methods? See how we choose the advice, including the original source links.

Affiliate links

Some guides link to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. If you follow one of these links, Amazon may set cookies to attribute the purchase; its own privacy notice applies on its site. Links never change our guidance.
